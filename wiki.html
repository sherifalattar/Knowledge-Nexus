Foreword

A connected body of work

Knowledge Nexus began as private notes — an attempt to understand how the UAE health-insurance system reasons, and where that reasoning departs from the Saudi market I had learned first. Research, comparison of references, and discussion with colleagues turned the notes into something with a structure: a reference for people who must navigate the clinical and financial logic of care in the UAE.

It is organised so that heavy material becomes navigable, around the UAE / DoH IR-DRG prospective-payment framework. Modern tools — artificial intelligence among them — are used for organisation and synthesis, never for authority. The authorities are named; where a passage carries none, the record says so rather than covering the gap.

What follows is the structure itself: every page, every section, and every point beneath them — numbered, stable, and citable.
